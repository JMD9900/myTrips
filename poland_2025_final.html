Day 2 – Aug 8 (Thu): Wawel Castle & Shopping

10:00
Depart hotel
10:30
🎟 Wawel Castle (09:30–17:00)
Historic royal complex dating back to the 14th century, featuring State Rooms, Wawel Cathedral, and the Dragon’s Den. Guided tours highlight Renaissance courtyards, royal tombs, and artistic masterpieces.
13:00
🚤 Vistula River Boat Ride (10:00–19:00)
Scenic cruise along the Vistula River offering panoramic views of Wawel Castle, the medieval stone bridge, and lush riverside parks. Includes live commentary in English and Polish, with optional hop-on/hop-off stops in the vibrant Kazimierz district.
14:00
🛍 Bonarka City Center (09:00–21:00)
One of Kraków’s largest malls with over 200 shops covering Polish designers and international brands, plus a cinema, food court, and regular cultural events.
18:00
🌆 Explore activities at Kraków Old Town
UNESCO World Heritage site with cobblestone streets, hidden courtyards, and vibrant nightlife. Spot local artisans, street musicians, and historic landmarks at every turn.
20:00
🍽 Dinner at Ottamam
Day-Specific Tips:
  • Must-book: Wawel Dragon's Den tickets (kids favorite!)
  • Bonarka: Teen-friendly shopping with international brands
  • Evening in Old Town: Street performers and live music common in summer
  • Effort level: Moderate (shopping and walking)

Day 7 – Aug 13 (Tue): Morskie Oko Adventure

Parking Reservation Required

You must book parking at least 3 days in advance through the official Tatra National Park website:

https://tpn.gov.pl/parkingi-morskie-oko

  • Booking opens 14 days before your visit date
  • Select "Parking Palenica Białczańska"
  • Print confirmation or save digital ticket - no reservation = no entry
10:00
Drive to 🌳 Palenica Białczańska Parking (official trailhead)
10:45
Hike to Morskie Oko (2-hour hike or horse cart)
Glacial lake at the foot of Rysy Peak surrounded by forested trails and dramatic mountain scenery. A must-see natural wonder in the Tatras.
13:00
🍽 Lunch at mountain shelter
Day-Specific Tips:
  • Parking hours: 7:00-19:00 (arrive before 9:00 AM)
  • Parking rates:
    • Passenger cars: 25 PLN (all day)
    • Minibuses: 50 PLN
    • Motorcycles: 10 PLN
  • Horse cart option: 100 PLN/person one way (cash only)
  • Trail condition: Paved but steep - hiking shoes essential
  • Effort level: High (9 km hike, 450m elevation)

Morskie Oko Specific Tips

  • Best Hiking Times: Start ascent by 10:00 AM to avoid crowds
  • Trail Conditions:
    • Paved road suitable for strollers/wheelchairs
    • Steeper section to Czarny Staw (requires proper shoes)
  • Weather Preparation:
    • Check forecast at tatrometeo.pl
    • Mountain weather changes rapidly - pack layers
  • Facilities:
    • Toilets at parking and mountain shelter
    • Potable water available at shelter

General Travel Tips

  • Clothing: Summer layers for daytime (20–28°C Kraków), warm jackets/raincoats for mountains, comfortable hiking shoes, swimwear for Energylandia
  • Tickets: Book Wawel Castle, Energylandia, Kasprowy Wierch Cable Car, and rafting at least 1 week ahead; Pre-book Wieliczka Salt Mine; Bachledka slide tickets (5 EUR) available online
  • Weather: Afternoon showers possible in August - carry an umbrella; Kraków ~25–28°C, Zakopane ~15–22°C; mountain peaks ~8–15°C
  • Effort Levels:
    • Easy Days: 1, 8, 9
    • Moderate Days: 2, 4, 5, 6
    • High Effort Days: 3 (park walking), 7 (hike)
  • Money & Connectivity: PLN cash needed for parking, horse carts, and local vendors; Orange/Play SIM cards available at airport
  • Food & Halal Dining: Hammam Szeherezada; Use Google Maps filter 'Halal' for more options
  • Driving & Parking: Zakopane parking fills by 10:00 AM; Fuel up at BP Zakopiańska before mountain drives
